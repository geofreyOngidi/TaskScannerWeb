Who it's for

Built for how Kenyan accounts actually get managed.

A KRA PIN is rarely handled by one person alone. TaxScanner mirrors that — an owner holds the PIN, and whoever actually does the filing gets exactly the access they need to do it.

Owner

Business & individual taxpayers

Hold every KRA PIN you run — company or personal — in one place, and see your position at a glance instead of digging through a folder of photos.

  • Register a PIN straight from WhatsApp, no paperwork
  • Hold multiple PINs under one login
  • Delegate filing without handing over the PIN itself
Account Manager

Accountants & bookkeepers

Get delegated access to exactly the PINs you manage, capture receipts on the client's behalf, and export a clean ledger in the format your workpapers already use.

  • Work across every client PIN you're delegated to
  • No registration step — start the moment you're invited
  • Export to CSV or PDF, per account or filtered by date
Finance Team

Companies with more than one PIN

Split the work properly: owners hold the PIN, managers do the filing, and every record shows exactly who captured it and how.

  • Invite colleagues onto specific accounts only
  • Full audit trail — AI-scanned vs. manually entered
  • One dashboard across every PIN the business runs
Features

Everything a receipt needs on its way into your records.

Nothing here requires an app install, a training session, or a new habit — it runs on the WhatsApp thread your team already has open.

WhatsApp capture

Send a photo of any receipt to the number you already message. It's logged the instant it arrives — before extraction even runs.

AI vision extraction

Supplier, PIN, amount, tax and date are read from the photo automatically — you always confirm before anything is saved.

Multi-PIN accounts

Hold a company PIN and a personal PIN side by side, and switch between them without switching logins.

Delegated access

Invite a colleague as an Account Manager on exactly the PINs you own — never automatically on all of them.

CSV & PDF exports

Download a single receipt, or every receipt in a date range, as a ledger-formatted CSV or paginated PDF report.

eTIMS-ready

Every record is structured to slot straight into KRA's eTIMS the moment that integration goes live — nothing to re-enter later.

How it works

Four steps, entirely inside your WhatsApp thread.

01

Say "Hi" to register

Enter your KRA PIN, or your National ID if you're registering as an individual — your details are verified automatically, in under a minute.

02

Send a photo of the receipt

Choose which account it belongs to (if you manage more than one), then attach the photo like you would to any other chat.

03

Confirm what was read

Supplier, PIN, amount, tax and date come back for your review. Edit anything that's off, then confirm — nothing saves without your say-so.

04

Find it later on the web

Log in here to search by PIN, filter by date, and export the whole ledger — or hand a clean export straight to your accountant.
